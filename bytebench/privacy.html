Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

ByteBench ("we," "us," or "our") operates bytebench.info. This page explains what information we collect when you visit, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. We've written this to be readable rather than just legally defensive.

Information We Collect

Automatically collected: When you visit our site, our hosting provider logs standard access data — your IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited, and timestamps. This data is used for server monitoring and security. We don't use it to identify you personally.

Newsletter signups: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. We use this only to send you content updates. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email we send.

Contact form submissions: If you contact us through our website, we collect the information you provide (name, email, message). We use this solely to respond to your inquiry.

Cookies: We use minimal cookies necessary for site functionality. We do not use advertising tracking cookies on our own behalf, though third-party services embedded in our site (see below) may set their own.

Third-Party Services

Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand general traffic patterns (which pages are popular, how long people spend reading). This data is aggregated and anonymized. Google's privacy policy governs how they handle this data.

Affiliate links: We participate in Amazon Associates and other affiliate programs. When you click an affiliate link, the retailer may place a cookie in your browser to track the referral. We don't have access to your purchase data beyond aggregate commission reporting. These programs have their own privacy policies.

We don't sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

Newsletter subscriber data is kept for as long as you're subscribed. Contact form submissions are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Server access logs are typically rotated after 30 days by our hosting provider.

Your Rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, email us at kushagra@kareerllc.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

If you're in the EU or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

ByteBench is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we've inadvertently collected information from a child, contact us and we'll delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our practices change. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
