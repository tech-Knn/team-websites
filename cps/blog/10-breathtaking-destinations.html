10 Breathtaking Destinations You Must Visit Before 2026

By TripsyTravel October 22, 2026 12 min read 18 Comments

From the wind-scoured spires of Patagonia to the electric pulse of Tokyo's neon streets, these ten destinations aren't just places to visit — they're experiences that will rewire the way you see the world.

We've spent the last three years chasing sunsets, missing flights, eating things we couldn't name, and sleeping in places ranging from five-star villas to crumbling hostels. What emerged from all that wandering is this list — a deeply personal, rigorously tested collection of destinations that we believe every curious traveler should experience at least once.

These aren't just "pretty places." Each one offers something distinct: a particular kind of silence, a cuisine that humbles every other food memory you have, a landscape so alien it makes you question what the word "earth" even means. They are transformative.

Torres del Paine at golden hour — Patagonia, Chile. Photo: Unsplash

The Full List of 10 Destinations

01

Patagonia, Chile & Argentina

End-of-the-world drama on an epic scale. Torres del Paine's granite spires, turquoise glacial lakes, and howling winds create a landscape that feels beyond cinematic. The W Trek is world-class but book early — permits run out fast.

Patagonia is one of those places that stays with you long after you've left. The sheer scale of it — the way the wind pushes back against every step — is humbling in the best possible way. We'd recommend spending at least 10 days here; rushing it is doing yourself a disservice.

02

Kyoto, Japan — Off-Season

Skip the cherry blossom crowds and visit in autumn (October–November) or deep winter. The bamboo grove, Fushimi Inari, and the ancient tea houses hit differently without 3,000 selfie sticks in frame.

03

The Faroe Islands, Denmark

Dramatics cliffs, green-turf-roofed houses, and a road that quite literally drives into a lake. Only 50,000 people live here but it might be the most spectacular landscape in Europe. Bring layers.

"Travel is the only thing you buy that makes you richer — and these ten destinations are worth every penny and every delayed flight to reach them."

The iconic Fushimi Inari shrine gates at dusk, Kyoto, Japan.

Getting There & Practical Tips

Best Time to Visit

Patagonia is best from November through March (Southern Hemisphere summer). The Faroe Islands are most dramatic (and most dramatic weatherwise) from May to September. Kyoto's shoulder seasons — March-April and October-November — offer the best balance of weather and manageable crowds.

Budget Expectations

  • Patagonia: Budget $120–200/day including accommodation, food, and park fees.
  • Kyoto: Can be done on $80–120/day staying in ryokans and eating at local izakayas.
  • Faroe Islands: Expect $150–250/day — it's not cheap, but it's not as expensive as Iceland.
04

Oaxaca, Mexico

The culinary capital of Mexico with mezcal, mole negro, tlayudas, and one of the most vibrant artisan craft scenes anywhere in Latin America. Day of the Dead here is unlike anything else on earth.

05

Namibia, Africa

The world's oldest desert meets some of its most surreal landscapes: red dunes at Sossusvlei, the desolate Skeleton Coast, and the dark-sky stargazing that will make you reconsider your life choices.

We could keep going — and honestly, the remaining five spots (Georgia, Raja Ampat, the Azores, Bhutan, and the Atacama Desert) each deserve their own dedicated article. Which they'll get. Subscribe to TripsyTravel and we'll bring each one to your inbox in full detail.

Crystal clear waters of the Maldives — a classic for good reason.

Final Thoughts

The world is simultaneously smaller and larger than it's ever been. Smaller because you can get to almost anywhere within 24 hours. Larger because there are still corners so remote, so raw, so genuinely unlike anywhere else, that they feel like they belong to a different planet.

These ten destinations are not just bucket-list checkboxes. They are chapters in a story you'll tell for the rest of your life. Go. Don't wait for the perfect moment. The perfect moment is right now.
