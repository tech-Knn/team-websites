How to Get the Best Deals on Expedia: Insider Tips That Actually Work

By Emma Torres October 24, 2026 7 min read

Expedia processes millions of bookings every day, but the best deals are rarely on the front page. If you know where to look and when to book, you can save hundreds on the exact same trip. Here's everything you need to know.

Expedia is the world's largest online travel agency by revenue, operating in over 70 countries and listing millions of hotels, flights, and car rentals. It's powerful, but its homepage is an advertising surface — the "deals" featured there are often not the best available prices. The real savings live deeper in the platform, and in the ways you use it.

Start with the Bundle

Expedia's biggest genuine advantage over competitors is its bundle pricing. When you book a flight and hotel together, Expedia often unlocks hotel rates that aren't available separately — sometimes 30–40% cheaper than booking the hotel alone. This is because hotels give Expedia better rates when they're part of a complete holiday package (they're guaranteed a longer stay and a motivated traveller).

Before booking anything separately, always run a bundle search: enter your destination, travel dates, and tick "Bundle & Save." Compare the bundled total to separate prices for the same flight and hotel. It's not always cheaper, but when it is, the difference is substantial.

Pro Tip

Add a rental car to your flight + hotel bundle. Expedia's triple-bundle deals often drop the car price to near-zero — you're sometimes paying less than $5/day for a car that would cost $30–50 standalone. Even if you don't plan to drive much, it can be worth it purely for the flexibility.

Join One Key — It's Free and Worth It

Expedia's loyalty programme, One Key, lets you earn OneKeyCash on every booking — essentially a cash rebate that applies to future bookings across Expedia, Hotels.com, and Vrbo. The earn rate starts at 2% but climbs to 4% (Silver) and 6% (Gold) once you hit annual spending thresholds.

The underrated aspect of One Key is that OneKeyCash earns across all three platforms. Book a villa on Vrbo for a family trip, and the credit applies to your next Expedia flight. For regular travellers, this cross-platform pooling adds up meaningfully.

Timing your flight searches right can unlock fares that disappear within hours.

When to Book Flights on Expedia

01

Domestic Flights: 1–3 Months Ahead

For domestic routes, prices tend to be lowest 4–6 weeks before departure. Booking too early (6+ months out) often means paying inflated launch fares. Set a price alert on Expedia and wait for the dip — they usually happen on Tuesday and Wednesday mornings.

02

International Flights: 2–6 Months Ahead

Long-haul fares have a wider sweet spot. The cheapest windows are typically 2–6 months out, with the lowest prices often appearing on a Tuesday or Wednesday. Avoid searching late on a Friday — prices are algorithmically inflated for weekend browsers. Clear your cookies or use incognito mode if you've searched the same route multiple times.

03

Use the Flexible Dates Calendar

Expedia's price calendar (toggle "Flexible dates" in the flight search) shows fares for an entire month at a glance. Shifting your departure by even one day can save $80–200 on popular routes. Flying out on a Tuesday or Wednesday is almost always cheaper than Thursday–Sunday.

Hotel Booking Strategies That Work

Filter by "Free Cancellation" First

Always start your hotel search with the "Free cancellation" filter enabled. This shows you every property that lets you cancel without penalty, usually up to 24–48 hours before arrival. Book your top pick, then keep monitoring prices. If the price drops (and it often does, especially for weekend stays booked midweek), cancel and rebook at the lower rate.

Look at the "Member Prices" Filter

Logged-in One Key members see exclusive Member Prices that aren't visible to guests. These can be 10–15% lower than the listed rate. If you're not a member, creating an account takes 2 minutes and is free — the savings start immediately on your first search.

The 10pm Rule for Last-Minute Hotels

For same-day bookings, check Expedia after 10pm local time at your destination. Hotels that still have unsold rooms for tonight will drop rates sharply in the last few hours. This strategy works best in major cities with high hotel density — it's unreliable in smaller towns or peak tourist seasons.

"Expedia is a tool. Like any tool, its usefulness depends entirely on how you use it. The travellers who pay full price are usually the ones who open the app and book whatever's on the front page."

Use Expedia as a Research Tool, Not Just a Booking Platform

One of Expedia's most underused features is its price comparison graph for hotels — hover over a property and you'll see its pricing history over the past 90 days. If a hotel is currently at its 90-day high, wait. If it's at its low, book immediately.

Expedia also aggregates reviews from multiple sources, which gives you a more balanced picture than any single-platform review system. Read the 3-star reviews specifically — they tend to be more balanced than 5-stars (often exaggerated) or 1-stars (often written in anger).

What Expedia Is Not Great For

In the interest of honesty: Expedia's customer service reputation is mixed when things go wrong. If a flight is cancelled or a hotel doesn't honour a booking, resolving it through Expedia as an intermediary can be slower than dealing directly with the airline or hotel. For complex trips with multiple moving parts, consider booking accommodation directly with hotels (after using Expedia to research the price baseline) to maintain a direct relationship.

Expedia also tends to show higher prices than specialist sites for niche categories. For luxury villa rentals, try Vrbo (owned by Expedia Group) or direct booking. For boutique guesthouses and ryokans, specialist platforms sometimes have better rates.

Quick-Reference Checklist

  • Always try the bundle (flight + hotel + optional car) before booking separately
  • Create a free One Key account to unlock Member Prices
  • Use the flexible dates calendar to find the cheapest departure day
  • Book refundable rates and monitor for price drops — rebook if cheaper
  • Search on Tuesday or Wednesday mornings for lowest fares
  • Use incognito mode if you've searched the same route multiple times
  • Check the 90-day price history graph before booking a hotel
  • For same-day hotels, search after 10pm for last-minute drops
