MakeMyTrip Explained: How to Book Flights, Hotels & Holidays in India Like a Pro

By Arjun Sharma October 27, 2026 9 min read

MakeMyTrip is India's largest online travel platform, handling everything from domestic flights to international holidays, hotel bookings, and train reservations. Used correctly, it's an incredibly powerful tool — but knowing the tricks separates the travellers who save thousands of rupees from those who overpay.

MakeMyTrip was founded in 2000 and went public on NASDAQ in 2010. Today it handles tens of millions of bookings annually across flights, hotels, holiday packages, buses, trains, and cab rentals. Its reach across India's domestic travel market is unmatched — for domestic flights in particular, it aggregates fares from every major Indian carrier alongside international airlines operating India routes.

Whether you're planning a long weekend in Goa, a family trip to Rajasthan, or an international holiday, here's how to use MakeMyTrip to get the best possible deal.

The myRewards Programme — Start Here

MakeMyTrip's loyalty programme, myRewards, gives you MMT Points on every booking — and these accumulate faster than you'd expect. Domestic flights earn points, hotel bookings earn points, even cab rides earn points. Accumulated points convert to wallet balance at ₹1 per point, which applies to future bookings.

More importantly, MakeMyTrip regularly runs double and triple points promotions tied to bank cards and wallet offers. Book on the right day with the right payment method and you can earn 3–5x points on a hotel booking. Follow MakeMyTrip's app notifications for these windows — they're frequently communicated as 24-hour flash offers.

Flight Booking: How to Get the Lowest Fares

01

Use the Flexible Date Feature for Domestic Flights

MakeMyTrip's flight calendar shows fares across a 30-day window in a single view. For popular routes like Delhi–Mumbai or Bangalore–Kolkata, shifting your departure by even one or two days can save ₹1,500–3,000 per person. Tuesday and Wednesday departures are consistently cheaper than weekend flights on most domestic routes.

02

Book 45–60 Days Ahead for Best Domestic Fares

Indian domestic flight pricing follows a predictable curve. Fares are lowest 45–60 days before departure, rise steeply in the final 2 weeks, and spike in the last 72 hours. For peak seasons (Diwali, Christmas, summer school holidays), book 90 days out. For general travel, the 6-week window is your sweet spot.

03

Compare "MakeMyTrip Price" vs. Direct Airline Price

MakeMyTrip's displayed fares include convenience fees. Always compare the total price (including all fees) against the airline's direct website. For budget carriers like IndiGo and SpiceJet, the direct price is sometimes marginally cheaper. For full-service carriers and international routes, MakeMyTrip frequently offers better rates and added value through its bundle offers and bank card discounts.

India's domestic air network connects hundreds of cities — MakeMyTrip is the most comprehensive aggregator for finding the best fares across all carriers.

Bank Card Offers: The Biggest Hidden Savings

This is where MakeMyTrip genuinely stands out versus direct booking. The platform runs a permanent roster of bank card instant discounts — typically 10–15% off on specific cards for flights and hotels. Common partners include HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, and Kotak cards.

Before booking anything on MakeMyTrip, check the "Bank Offers" section on the payment page. If your card is listed, you may get an immediate discount of ₹500–2,500 on a mid-range hotel booking with zero additional action required.

Money-Saving Tip

MakeMyTrip frequently runs "Pay with UPI and save" offers. Paying via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, or any UPI app) on select bookings gives an additional cashback of ₹200–500 directly to your UPI-linked account. These offers rotate monthly — check the app's "Offers" tab before making any payment.

Hotel Booking Strategies

Use the "Tonight's Deal" Feature

MakeMyTrip's Tonight's Deal section shows deeply discounted same-day hotel rates — typically 30–50% below the standard price for that night. This is one of the best features on the platform and is almost never discussed. If your plans allow for last-minute bookings, check this section first before running a standard search. Hotels that still have unsold rooms tonight slash prices dramatically to fill them.

Sort by "Price, Low to High" After Filtering

MakeMyTrip's default hotel sort is "Popularity" which, like most booking platforms, prioritises commercial performance over value. Once you've set your filters (star rating, area, amenities), switch the sort to "Price: Low to High." This surfaces genuinely competitive options that the algorithm buries. Read at least 10 reviews before booking anything under ₹2,000/night — the quality range is wide.

The Free Cancellation Filter

Always enable "Free Cancellation" when searching hotels. MakeMyTrip lists thousands of properties where you can cancel without penalty up to 24 hours before check-in. Book your top pick, then monitor prices. If the price drops — which it frequently does closer to the date for business hotels in major cities — cancel and rebook at the lower rate.

Holiday Packages: When They're Worth It

MakeMyTrip's holiday packages (flight + hotel combinations sold as a bundle) are genuinely competitive for popular Indian domestic destinations — Goa, Kerala, Himachal Pradesh, Andaman — and for international destinations like Thailand, Singapore, Bali, and Dubai, where volume pricing gives them strong rates.

The package model works best when: you're visiting a destination for the first time and don't know which area to stay in (MakeMyTrip's curated packages put you in well-located hotels), or when you want a hassle-free trip where transfers and sometimes tours are included.

It works less well for independent travellers who want specific hotels, niche destinations, or maximum flexibility. For these cases, book components individually and use MakeMyTrip purely for the flights.

International Bookings on MakeMyTrip

MakeMyTrip handles international bookings for 1 million+ hotels globally and almost all major international routes. For Indians travelling internationally, MakeMyTrip has a specific advantage: it displays prices in INR from the start, integrates with Indian bank offer discounts that don't apply on foreign OTAs, and has Indian customer service that understands the context of travel documentation requirements specific to Indian passport holders.

For popular Southeast Asia, Middle East, and European routes, compare MakeMyTrip's bundled price against booking flights directly on the airline and using a global OTA for hotels. For Thailand, Dubai, and Singapore packages in particular, MakeMyTrip's bundle prices are frequently best-in-class for the Indian market.

"MakeMyTrip's real edge is its bank offer ecosystem and its understanding of the Indian traveller. For trips within Asia and the Middle East especially, it's often the single best place to book."

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Ignoring the "Add-ons" screen: When booking flights, MakeMyTrip's add-on screen (meals, baggage, seat selection) often contains pre-ticked items. Uncheck anything you don't need before completing payment.
  • Not checking the cancellation policy carefully: "Partially refundable" and "fully refundable" are not the same thing. Read the exact cancellation terms before booking, especially for hotels with complex fee structures.
  • Booking at full price without checking offers: There is almost always an active bank or wallet offer on MakeMyTrip. If you're booking without applying one, you're probably overpaying.
  • Using MakeMyTrip for train bookings without comparing IRCTC: IRCTC (Indian Railways' official portal) often has better availability and no OTA markup. Use MakeMyTrip for train bookings only if IRCTC is showing waitlisted tickets.

MakeMyTrip vs. Competitors

The two main alternatives to MakeMyTrip in the Indian market are Cleartrip (now owned by Flipkart) and EaseMyTrip. Cleartrip often has comparable flight fares and a cleaner interface. EaseMyTrip charges zero convenience fees on certain booking types, which can make it slightly cheaper for flights. The practical recommendation: check all three for major bookings, but use MakeMyTrip as your primary platform for its superior loyalty programme and bank offer ecosystem.
