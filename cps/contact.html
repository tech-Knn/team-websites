Frequently Asked Questions

About Us ›
Can I write for TripsyTravel?
Yes! We love hearing from passionate travelers. Send us a pitch with your proposed topic, a short outline, and 2–3 links to your previous writing to kushagra@kareerllc.com with the subject "Write for Us".
Do you accept sponsored posts?
We do accept sponsored content and brand partnerships, but only with travel brands we genuinely believe in. All sponsored content is clearly labeled. Reach out to kushagra@kareerllc.com with details about your brand and campaign.
How often do you publish new content?
We publish 3–5 new travel guides and stories every week. Subscribe to our newsletter to get the best ones delivered straight to your inbox every Friday morning.
I found an error in one of your articles — how do I report it?
Travel information changes fast and we appreciate the help keeping our guides accurate. Use the contact form above with the subject "Report an Error" and include the article title and what needs updating. We'll review and update promptly.
Do you offer personalised travel planning?
We don't offer custom itinerary planning as a service, but our guides are designed to be comprehensive enough to plan your trip with. If you have a specific question, drop us a message and we'll do our best to point you in the right direction.
How do affiliate links work on TripsyTravel?
Some links in our articles are affiliate links — if you book or buy through them, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products and services we've used ourselves or genuinely trust.
