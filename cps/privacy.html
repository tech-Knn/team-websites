Welcome to TripsyTravel ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit www.tripsytravel.info. By using our website, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name and email address when you submit our contact form or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, browser type, and referring URLs, collected automatically via analytics tools.
  • Cookies — small files placed on your device to improve your browsing experience and track site performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and messages
  • Send newsletters and travel content (only if you opted in)
  • Improve our website content and user experience
  • Analyse traffic and understand how visitors use our site
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Cookies

We use cookies to personalise content and analyse our traffic. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, some parts of our site may not function properly without cookies.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services including:

  • Google Analytics — to track site usage. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Affiliate links — we participate in affiliate programmes (e.g., Booking.com, Amazon). Clicking these links may result in a small commission to us at no extra cost to you. All affiliate links are disclosed.
  • Unsplash — images are served via Unsplash CDN.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict our processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at kushagra@kareerllc.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
