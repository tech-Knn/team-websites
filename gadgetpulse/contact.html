Frequently Asked Questions

About Us ›
How do you decide what to review?
We prioritise products that a large number of our readers are actively considering buying. We track reader questions, search trends, and product launches. We also accept reader requests — if enough people ask about a product, we'll track it down and test it.
Do you accept sponsored reviews?
We do not accept payment for reviews. Brands can send us products to test, but they have no influence over our scores or conclusions. Paid partnerships (sponsored content) are always clearly labelled as such and kept separate from our review methodology.
Can I submit a product for review?
Yes — brands and manufacturers can submit products for review consideration. Use the contact form above with "Product Review Request" as the subject. Include the product name, specs, and why you think our readers would benefit from the review. We can't guarantee coverage of every submission, but we read every pitch.
How do affiliate links work on GadgetPulse?
Some links in our articles are affiliate links — if you click and buy through them, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This helps fund the site. We never let affiliate arrangements influence our recommendations. We only link to products we'd genuinely recommend regardless of commission.
Do you offer consulting or tech advice?
We don't offer formal consulting services, but our guides are designed to be comprehensive enough to make informed decisions independently. If you have a specific question that isn't covered in our articles, feel free to drop us a message and we'll do our best to point you in the right direction.
I found incorrect information in a review.
Tech specs and prices change frequently, and we genuinely appreciate being corrected. Use the contact form with the subject "Report an Error" and include the article title, the specific claim in question, and the correct information with a source if possible. We'll review and update it promptly.
