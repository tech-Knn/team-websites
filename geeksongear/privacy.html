Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Geeks on Gear ("we," "us," or "our") operates geeksongear.info. This policy explains what data we collect when you use our site, why we collect it, and how you can exercise your rights.

Information We Collect

Server logs: Our hosting provider automatically records standard access data — IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and request timestamps. We use this for security monitoring and to identify broken links. We don't use it to personally identify visitors.

Newsletter subscriptions: If you subscribe, we collect your email address and use it only to send content updates. You can unsubscribe with one click at any time.

Contact form submissions: If you message us through the site, we collect the information you provide (name, email, message content) and use it to respond to you.

Cookies: We use cookies for basic site functionality. We don't run our own ad tracking. Third-party services we use may set their own cookies subject to their own policies.

Third-Party Services

Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand general traffic — which categories are popular, how people navigate the site. Data is aggregated and anonymized.

We participate in Amazon Associates and other affiliate programs. Clicking an affiliate link may result in the retailer placing a referral cookie in your browser. We don't receive your personal purchase data beyond aggregate commission reports.

Data Retention

Newsletter email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe. Contact messages are kept for up to 12 months. Server logs are rotated by our hosting provider after approximately 30 days.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Email kushagra@kareerllc.com and we'll respond within 30 days. EU/UK residents may also contact their local data protection authority.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we've done so inadvertently, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance.
