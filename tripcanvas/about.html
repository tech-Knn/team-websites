About TripCanvas

We're a team of wanderers who believe every journey deserves to be painted with detail, honesty, and a little bit of wonder.

Our Story

Started by travelers, for travelers

TripCanvas launched in 2023 when three friends — after a chaotic but unforgettable trip across Southeast Asia — realized that most travel content online was either too generic or too polished to be useful.

We set out to build a place where real experiences live: the missed trains, the hidden local restaurants, the budget hacks that actually work, and the itineraries built by people who just returned from the destination.

What We Stand For

Honest Reviews

No sponsored fluff. We tell you what's great and what's overhyped — with evidence.

Budget-Friendly

We cover destinations across all budgets, with real cost breakdowns and money-saving tips.

Community-Driven

We feature real readers' stories and update guides based on community feedback.
